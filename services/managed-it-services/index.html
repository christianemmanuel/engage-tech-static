What managed IT actually covers

Managed IT means we take ownership of your day-to-day technology: the help desk your team calls, the devices they work on, the network they connect through and the servers behind it. You get a fixed monthly cost and a named team, instead of an hourly invoice that grows the worse things get.

Most clients come to us running a mix of ageing on-prem hardware and Microsoft 365, with patching handled by whoever remembers. Our first ninety days are spent making that estate boring: enrolled, patched, backed up and monitored.

FAQ

Questions buyers ask before they call

How is managed IT priced?
A flat monthly fee per user, set after the assessment. Servers and network devices are priced separately where they exist. There is no per-ticket charge — unlimited support is the point of the model.
Can you work alongside our internal IT person?
Yes — that is our co-managed model. We typically take the help desk, patching and monitoring so your internal lead can focus on projects and vendor relationships.
How long does onboarding take?
Two to three weeks for most 15–100 seat environments. Critical security gaps — MFA, failing backups, unpatched internet-facing systems — are addressed in week one rather than waiting for the full rollout.
Are your engineers based in the US?
Every engineer is US-based and Microsoft-certified. There is no offshore first line and no script-reading tier between you and someone who can actually fix the problem.
