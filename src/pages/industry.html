Industry challenges

What makes healthcare IT different

A generalist MSP will keep your laptops patched. It won't know what happens to a practice when the EHR is unreachable at 8:40am with a full waiting room.

Clinical downtime is patient risk

An outage isn't an inconvenience — it's cancelled appointments, paper charting and a backlog that lasts a week.

PHI everywhere

Protected health information sits in the EHR, in email, in imaging systems and on the tablet a clinician took home.

Audit evidence, not assurances

Risk analysis, access logs and BAAs have to exist as documents before anyone asks for them.

High staff turnover

Locums, residents and per-diem staff need access on day one and removed the day they leave.

Legacy clinical devices

Imaging and diagnostic equipment running unsupported operating systems that can't simply be patched.

Support outside office hours

Clinics open at 7am and urgent care runs weekends. IT that starts at nine doesn't cover the risk window.

Healthcare FAQ

What practices ask us first

Will you sign a Business Associate Agreement?
Yes — a BAA is part of standard onboarding for every healthcare client, executed before we touch any system that can reach PHI.
Can you support our EHR directly?
We manage everything the EHR depends on — identity, endpoints, network, connectivity and backup — and act as your liaison with the EHR vendor's own support. We don't replace the vendor's clinical application support, and we say so up front.
What about imaging equipment we can't patch?
Those devices get network-segmented and monitored rather than patched. The compensating controls are documented so they stand up in a risk analysis.
