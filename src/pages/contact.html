Before you send

Questions people ask on this page

Is the consultation really free?
Yes — the discovery call and the conversation that follows cost nothing. A full environment assessment is a paid engagement, and we tell you the price before you commit to it.
How quickly will someone get back to me?
Within one business hour between 8am and 6pm ET. Outside those hours you'll hear from us first thing the next business morning.
We're only 10 people. Are we too small?
No. We work with teams from around 10 users upward. Below that, co-managed or project work is usually a better fit than a full managed agreement, and we'll say so.
Do we have to leave our current IT provider first?
Not to talk to us. Many clients start with a co-managed arrangement or a single project. If you do transition, we handle the handover directly with the outgoing provider.
