Raleigh FAQ

Local questions we get asked

Do you actually come on site in Raleigh?
Yes. Our engineers are Raleigh-based and we target two hours on site for critical incidents inside a 45-mile radius. Most issues resolve faster remotely, and we'll tell you which is which rather than billing a visit that wasn't needed.
Which nearby areas do you cover?
Cary, Morrisville, Apex, Durham, Chapel Hill, Wake Forest, Garner, Holly Springs and Research Triangle Park, plus Charlotte through our second service area.
Can you help us answer a client security questionnaire?
This is one of the most common reasons Triangle firms call us. We map your current controls against the framework being asked about — usually SOC 2, NIST CSF or CIS — and produce the evidence, then close the gaps.
